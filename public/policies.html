01Policy

Privacy Policy

(“we”, “us”) operates (“the Platform”). This policy explains how personal data in church directories is handled. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the equivalent laws in other countries where churches use the Platform.

1.1

Who is responsible

Each subscribing church decides why and how its members' data is used and is responsible for it as the data fiduciary (or “controller”). We act as a data processor: we store and process directory data only to provide the Platform to that church, under its instructions.

For data about church administrators' own accounts, billing and use of our website, we are responsible directly.

We do not ask for identity-document numbers, financial accounts, caste or offerings, and we advise churches not to collect them.

1.3

How the data is used

  • To build, display, search and export each church's directory (web view, family index, PDF and print).
  • To let members find and contact families in their own church, organise prayer groups, and respond to genuine needs such as blood donation requests.
  • To send account emails such as invitations, password resets and notices about changes to a directory.
  • To keep the Platform secure, fix problems and provide support.

We never sell directory data, use it for advertising, train AI models on it, or combine it across churches.

1.4

Consent

Churches must obtain clear consent from each family before adding them, and tell them what will be shown and to whom. Every adult member can choose to hide their own phone number, email, blood group, occupation or photo, and can withdraw consent at any time.

Children: under-18 members are added only with verifiable consent from a parent or guardian. The Platform does not show contact details for children, does not allow children to hold accounts, and does not track or profile them.

1.5

Who can see it

  • Members see only the directory of the church they belong to, and only the fields their church makes visible.
  • Church administrators manage their own church's records.
  • Our staff access data only when needed for support or security, under confidentiality obligations, and every access is logged.
  • Service providers that we use to run the Platform (cloud hosting, email delivery, payments). They are bound by contract to protect the data and use it only for us. A current list is available at .
  • Authorities, only where required by law, and we will tell the church unless prohibited.

No member of one church can see another church's directory.

1.6

Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest; passwords stored as one-way hashes.
  • Each church's data is logically separated; access is based on role.
  • Exports and shared links can be password-protected and watermarked with the member's name.
  • Regular backups, monitoring and security updates.
  • If a breach affects personal data, we will notify the affected church and the relevant authority without undue delay, as required by law, and help the church inform its members.
1.7

Your rights

Members may ask to access, correct, update or erase their data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for them in case of death or incapacity. Most changes can be made from your own profile. Otherwise, contact your church administrator, or us at , and we will pass the request to your church and help it respond within 30 days.

1.8

Retention

Data is kept while the church subscribes and the member remains in its directory. When a member is removed, their entry is deleted from the live directory straight away and from backups within 90 days. When a church closes its account, it can export its data for 30 days, after which all its data is permanently deleted within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer. Copies already printed or downloaded remain the church's responsibility.

1.9

Cookies

We use only essential cookies to keep you signed in and secure. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

1.10

Where data is stored

Data is hosted with reputable cloud providers. Where data is stored or processed outside the country of the church, we do so only as permitted by applicable law and with appropriate safeguards.

1.11

Contact & Grievance Officer

, , · . We acknowledge complaints within 7 days and aim to resolve them within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India or the data protection authority in your country.

We may update this policy and will notify churches at least 30 days before significant changes take effect.

03Guidelines

Community Guidelines

Every name in a church directory belongs to a family who trusted their church with it. These guidelines apply to every church and every member on . They ask us to treat one another's details the way we would want our own treated: with kindness, discretion and respect.

3.1

Reach out with care

Please do

  • Introduce yourself and your family when contacting someone for the first time.
  • Call or message at reasonable hours unless it is urgent.
  • Ask before visiting someone's home.
  • Use birthdays and anniversaries to send greetings and remember people in prayer.

Please don't

  • Keep messaging someone who hasn't replied.
  • Forward chain messages, rumours or unverified news.
  • Pass on someone's number without asking them.
  • Contact children directly; always go through their parents.
3.2

Blood donation requests

Where a church shows blood groups, use them only for a genuine, current medical need. Share the patient's name, hospital and units needed, accept it graciously if someone can't donate, and let donors know once the need is met.

3.3

Keep it within the church

Don't screenshot, photograph, download or forward directory pages, and don't post them on social media, even in private groups. What you learn about where someone lives, their family or their work is not for gossip or comment.

3.4

No selling, no soliciting

A church directory is a place of fellowship, not a customer list. Don't use it to promote a business, product, service or appeal. If you have something to share with the congregation, ask your church leadership to announce it through the proper channels.

3.5

Prayer groups and committees

Group leaders and conveners may contact the families in their group about meetings and church activities. Keep group conversations focused on fellowship, and share meeting details only within the group.

3.6

Photos and profile content

Upload only photos of your own family, that everyone pictured is happy to share. Keep profile details accurate, respectful and free of advertising or links.

3.7

Times of loss or difficulty

When a family is grieving or facing hardship, follow the lead of your clergy and the family before sharing news. Let the family decide what is shared and when.

3.8

For church administrators

  • Add a family only after they have agreed, and honour requests to hide or remove details promptly.
  • Give administrator access only to people the church has authorised, and remove it when their role ends.
  • Share exports and printed copies only with current members.
3.9

Reporting a concern

If you receive unwanted contact through a directory, or see one being misused, tell your church administrator or clergy, or use “Report a concern” in the app, or write to . Reports are handled discreetly. Breaking these guidelines may lead to a warning, loss of directory access, or removal by the church or by us.
